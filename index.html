Commission System

Hala Agency Commission

Commission Table

Agency Commission Level Total Income (past 30 days) Commission Rate
D 0 - 2M 4%
C 2M - 10M 8%
B 10M - 50M 12%
A 50 - 150M 16%
S 150M+ 20%

Notes:

1. Total income = Your agency income + Each of your sub-agencies income

2. Agency ratio will be credited in real-time when the host receives a gift.

Commission from Host

Agent earns a percentage of their hosts’ total earnings (excluding their platform rewards, rankings, tasks, etc.)

Formula: How to calculate commission from only hosts:
1. Total Income × commission rate

Commission from Sub-agent

Agents also earn a percentage of their sub-agents’ total earnings.

Formula: How to calculate commission from sub-agents:
(Your agency ratio − Sub-agency ratio rate) × Your total income.

Commission Calculation Example:

  • 1. Agency A’s total valid income = Agency A + Sub-agency B + C + D + E + F (10M + 20M + 70M + 20M + 30M + 80M) = 230M
    Therefore, Agency A’s ratio rate is 20% (refer to the table in section 1)
  • 2. Agency B’s total valid income = B + D + E 20M + 20M + 30M = 70M
    Therefore, Agency B’s ratio rate is 16%
  • 3. Agency C’s total valid income = C + F 70M + 80M = 150M
    Therefore, Agency C’s ratio rate is 20%
  • Calculation: 1. Agency A's ratio from Agency B = (20% − 16%) × (20M + 20M + 30M) = 2.8M
    Agency A's ratio from Agency C = (20% − 20%) × (70M + 80M) = 0
    Therefore, Agency A's total ratio from sub-agencies is 2.8M + 0 = 2.8M = $280 USD

    2. Agency B's ratio from Agency D = (16% − 12%) × 20M = 0.8M
    Agency B's ratio from Agency E = (16% − 12%) × 30M = 1.2M
    Therefore, Agency B's total ratio from sub-agencies is 0.8M + 1.2M = 2M = $200 USD

    3. Agency C's ratio from Agency F = (20% − 16%) × 80M = 3.2M
    Therefore, Agency C's total ratio from sub-agencies is 3.2M = $320 USD

    4. Since Agency D, E, and F have no sub-agency, they don’t earn sub-agency ratio.

Policy & Integrity

Hala Live Agency Rules

01

1. Agents are not allowed to spam "join agency" invitations to hosts and users.

02

2. Agents are not allowed to recruit hosts from other agents on the platform.

03

3. It is prohibited to promote other Apps.

04

4. Hala Live strictly prohibits any fraudulent activities. Any violations will result in permanent account suspension.

05

5. If the agent does not process the request to leave the agency within 7 days, the host will automatically leave the agency.

06

6. Hosts who have been with the agency for less than 24 hours can leave the agency immediately after submitting their first request.

07

7. After submitting a request to leave, regardless of whether the request is approved, the hosts cannot submit another request within 30 days.
